ColdHook — Paths: Gradient Stroke + Section Bleed

3 prompts · background-paths.tsx · hero.tsx · app/page.tsx

What this does:

The animated path strokes currently feel disconnected from the hero background because they are pure white on a dark-to-indigo radial gradient — two different color temperatures fighting each other.

Fix 1 — Gradient stroke: Add an SVG linearGradient that goes from white at the upper-left origin (matching the dark top of the hero) to a soft periwinkle mid-point, dissolving into #3b1fa8 (the exact purple at the hero gradient edge) at the lower-right. The paths now look like they are part of the background glow rather than floating over it.

Fix 2 — Section bleed: The paths currently live inside the hero's overflow-hidden section and are clipped at the hero boundary, creating a hard visual cut. Move them up to app/page.tsx as an absolute layer behind <Hero>, <TickerBar>, and the upper half of <Features>. The diagonal sweep continues naturally through the section boundary and dissolves about halfway down the features grid.

Run in order: background-paths.tsx first, then hero.tsx, then app/page.tsx.

in both axes — correct behaviour for the section-bleed layout added in prompt 3.
