ColdHook — Tour Bubble Left Position

2 prompts · onboarding/tour.tsx + compose/page.tsx

Problem: On step 6 ("Your personalized email"), the tour bubble sits over the top-right of the output panel, covering the score and email content it is supposed to showcase. Above/below placement cannot fix this because the spotlight element (the full right-column result group) is too tall — the bubble always lands on top of it.

Fix: Add a position: "left" option to the tour. When set, the bubble floats to the LEFT of the spotlight element, vertically centred, with a rightward-pointing caret. On this layout the left panel is always dimmed by the backdrop anyway, so the bubble sits harmlessly over dead space while the entire right output column stays unobstructed. If the left side has no room (spotlight is near the left edge), fall back to right side placement.

Files: (1) src/components/onboarding/tour.tsx — add left/right placement logic and caret. (2) src/app/(app)/compose/page.tsx — set position: "left" on the email-result step (index 5).
