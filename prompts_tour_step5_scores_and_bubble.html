Prompt 2 — Bubble never covers the spotlight:

The tour bubble sometimes sits on top of the section it is highlighting. Root cause: (a) BUBBLE_HEIGHT_ESTIMATE is too small (160px) so the "above" placement doesn't reserve enough clearance; (b) the viewport clamp forces bubbleTop back into the spotlight when the element is near the top of the screen; (c) there is no post-clamp overlap check. Fix: increase the estimate to 220px, rewrite the above/below decision to pick whichever side has more available space, and add a final anti-overlap guard that detects intersection and pushes the bubble to the opposite side.

The tour bubble sometimes overlaps the element it is spotlighting. Three root causes:

1. BUBBLE_HEIGHT_ESTIMATE = 160 is too small. Real bubbles are ~200–220px tall.
   When placing the bubble above the spotlight, the bottom of the bubble can
   intrude into the top of the element.

2. The above/below decision uses a fixed heuristic (element below 60% viewport).
   It does not check how much actual space exists above vs. below.

3. The viewport clamp (bubbleTop = Math.max(12, ...)) can push a bubble that was
   calculated for the "above" position back down into the spotlight when the
   element is near the top of the screen. There is no post-clamp overlap check.
